These maps turn Kontur's population data, counted in H3 hexagons of about 0.74 km² each, into 3D terrain: the taller the column, the more people live there. I rendered them in R with Rayshader, starting with Bangladesh and its divisions and then moving across South Asia.

The same data now runs an interactive 3D population density map of 33 countries in the browser. Fly between regions, tilt the view and hover any column to see how many people live there.

What the project covers:

  • Spatial data in R with sf: reading H3 hexagons and turning them into a raster matrix.
  • Ray-traced 3D renders with Rayshader, with titles and credits added in magick.
  • A browser explorer built with deck.gl and MapLibre, generated from the same country config.
  • Data Analytics
  • R Programming
  • Rayshader
  • 3D Plot
  • deck.gl
